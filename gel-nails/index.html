Good to know

Gel nails, at a glance

How long does gel last?

A professional gel manicure typically lasts 2–3 weeks without chipping. Gel extensions and overlays may need a refill every 2–4 weeks depending on nail growth and aftercare.

Gel vs acrylic vs soft gel

Gel extensions use a gel-based builder for a glossy, flexible finish. Acrylic offers strong structure with a classic sculpted look. Soft gel is lighter and soak-off friendly. Not sure which suits you? Message us on WhatsApp.

Making a gel set last

Apply cuticle oil daily, wear gloves for cleaning, avoid using nails as tools and never peel product off at home. These simple habits can add 7–10 extra days of wear to any gel set.

Safe removal

Never bite, peel or forcibly remove gel — this damages the natural nail plate. Professional gel polish removal uses the method appropriate to the formula applied.
