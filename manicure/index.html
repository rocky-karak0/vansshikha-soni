Manicure

The Manicure Menu

Classic colour, timeless French and quick mini manicures — plus gel sets and nail art to finish the look.

All Services

The application category holds the two services clients return to most often: a simple polished finish, and the classic French look with a natural-looking base. Tip shape, colour and base shade can be customised after consultation.

Short on time? The mini manicure category covers nail cut + file for a tidy, natural shape and nail cut + polish for trimming with a polished colour finish — a quick refresh without an elaborate nail-art design.

Prefer longer wear? Gel colour sets — cat-eye, marble, glitter, ombré, chrome — sit alongside the classics, and per-finger nail art starts from the official menu rate if you want an accent.

Manicure options

Choose your finish

Classic

A clean colour application for a polished everyday look — a simple, versatile finish for clients who prefer understated nails. Confirm with the studio whether you would like regular polish or gel.

French

A defined tip over a natural-looking base — elegant and neat for everyday wear and special occasions, with tip shape, colour and base shade customised after consultation.

Mini manicure

Nail cut + file for a tidy, natural-looking shape, or nail cut + polish for trimming with a polished colour finish. A quick refresh without an elaborate nail-art design.

Add-ons

Per-finger nail art starts from the official menu rate, and advance gel sets add an effect — magnetic cat-eye, marble, glitter, blended ombré or reflective chrome.
