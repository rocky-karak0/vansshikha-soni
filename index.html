Expert Advice

Nail Care Guide

Professional tips from our luxury nail atelier to keep your manicure flawless for longer.

01

How Long Does a Gel Manicure Last?

A professionally applied gel manicure typically lasts 2–3 weeks without chipping. Gel extensions and overlays may need a refill every 2–4 weeks depending on nail growth and aftercare. Book a gel refill at Vansshikha Soni Nail Atelier to maintain your set between full appointments.

02

Gel vs Acrylic vs Soft Gel Extensions

Gel extensions use a gel-based builder for a glossy, flexible finish. Acrylic extensions offer strong structure with a classic sculpted look. Soft gel extensions are lighter and soak-off friendly. Not sure which suits you? Message us on WhatsApp — we'll recommend the best system for your natural nail condition.

03

How to Make Your Nail Art Last Longer

Apply cuticle oil daily, wear gloves for cleaning, avoid using nails as tools, and never peel gel polish at home. Schedule a refill before lifting begins. These simple habits can add 7–10 extra days of wear to any gel set or nail extension.

04

Safe Nail Extension Removal

Never bite, peel or forcibly remove extensions — this damages the natural nail plate. Professional removal at our atelier uses the correct method for your system (gel, acrylic or soft gel). Gel polish removal starts from ₹150; extension removal from ₹300.
