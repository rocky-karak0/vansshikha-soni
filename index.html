Before you book

Our luxury nail atelier is based in India. The full studio address is shared privately on booking confirmation. For appointments, directions or a studio tour, message us directly on WhatsApp at +91 78389 13373.

Use the booking form on our website to pick a service, preferred date and time — then send the request on WhatsApp. Vansshikha personally confirms availability. Nothing is booked until you receive a confirmation reply. Walk-ins are subject to availability.

We offer 23 professional nail services across 8 categories: classic & French application, advance gel sets (cat-eye, marble, glitter, ombre, chrome), custom nail art, gel & acrylic extensions, overlays, refills, mini manicures and safe removal. View the full menu with official rates →

Gel extensions start from ₹900–₹1,000 on our official menu. Acrylic extensions are ₹1,100–₹1,200, soft gel extensions ₹800–₹900, and gum gel extensions ₹1,500–₹1,700. Final pricing is confirmed on WhatsApp based on length, design and product system. See extension details →

A professional gel manicure typically lasts 2–3 weeks. With proper aftercare — cuticle oil daily, gloves for chores, no peeling — many clients get even longer wear. Book a gel refill before lifting starts to extend the life of your set.

Please message us on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your appointment if you need to reschedule. Same-day cancellations may affect future booking priority. We understand life happens — just communicate early so we can offer the slot to another client.

When done professionally, yes. We use the correct removal method for your system — never forced peeling. Gel polish removal starts from ₹150; extension removal from ₹300. Never remove gel or extensions at home by biting or scraping — this damages the nail plate permanently.
