About this service

A nail-length enhancement created with an extension system and a gel-based building product, giving added length with a shaped, finished appearance.

What the service involves

Length, shape, structure and suitability should be assessed by the nail technician.

How the appointment works

  • Length, shape, structure and suitability are assessed by the nail technician first.
  • The extension system and gel-based building product are applied to create the agreed length and shape.
  • Final structure and finish are checked with you before the set is completed.

Suitable looks

Added length and a shaped, finished nail appearance.

Key features

  • Added length with a shaped, finished look
  • Built with an extension system and gel-based product
  • Listed under NAIL EXTENSION

Care & maintenance

Apply cuticle oil daily, wear gloves for cleaning, avoid using your nails as tools and never peel product off at home. If anything begins to lift, book a refill or contact the studio rather than removing it yourself. General guidance from our atelier — your technician will confirm the aftercare that suits your specific product system.
