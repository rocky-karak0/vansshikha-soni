Nail Shapes

Choose Your Nail Shape

Square, oval, almond, coffin, stiletto — how each shape reads, and how to choose one that suits your nails.

Book AppointmentAll Services

Shape changes the whole feel of a set. Shorter everyday shapes read neat and practical; longer tapered shapes read more dramatic. The right choice depends on your nail bed, how you use your hands and the look you want.

At the atelier, length, shape, structure and suitability are assessed by the nail technician first — for extensions especially — so the result works with your natural nail condition. French tips can be customised by tip shape, colour and base shade.

Whatever the shape, the same aftercare applies: cuticle oil daily, gloves for cleaning, no using nails as tools and professional removal at the end of a set.

Shape guide

Six classic shapes

Square

A straight, flat tip with parallel sides — a clean, structured look that reads modern and works well on shorter lengths.

Squoval

Square with softened corners — the practical middle ground, neat on short to medium nails.

Oval

A soft, rounded tip that follows the nail — a classic, understated shape for everyday wear.

Almond

Gently tapered sides with a rounded tip — an elegant, elongating shape that suits medium to longer nails.

Coffin (ballerina)

Tapered sides with a flat squared-off tip — a statement shape that shows off colour and nail art.

Stiletto

A sharply pointed tip — the most dramatic shape, best supported by extensions.
