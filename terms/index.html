Terms & Booking

How appointments, pricing and rescheduling work at the atelier.

Book AppointmentAll Services

Appointments are requested through the website or WhatsApp. Nothing is booked until Vansshikha personally confirms availability in a reply — walk-ins are subject to availability.

Rates shown on the site are the official menu rates with M and R labels exactly as listed; their meaning is being confirmed by the studio. Final pricing is confirmed on WhatsApp before your appointment.

Policy

The essentials

Requests, not instant bookings

Use the booking form or WhatsApp to request a slot. Vansshikha confirms availability personally — nothing is booked until you receive a confirmation reply.

Pricing

M and R labels are shown exactly as on the official menu. Final pricing is confirmed on WhatsApp before your appointment; complex nail art may need a separate quote.

Rescheduling

Please message on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your appointment if you need to reschedule. Same-day cancellations may affect future booking priority.

Walk-ins & address

Walk-ins are subject to availability. The studio is based in India and the full address is shared privately on booking confirmation.
