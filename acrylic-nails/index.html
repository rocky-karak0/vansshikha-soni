Acrylic Nails

Acrylic Nails

Sculpted length and structure — acrylic extensions, overlays, refills and safe removal, with the official rate on every service.

All Services

Acrylic is the structured side of the menu: an acrylic system built over the natural nail to add length and shape, an acrylic coating that keeps your own length with a reinforced surface, and the refills that maintain a set as it grows.

Maintenance matters. Refills address new nail growth and rebalance the set, and damaged or lifting enhancements may need repair or removal instead. Book before lifting starts — sets are usually refilled every 2–4 weeks.

Removal is professional, never forced: different systems need different methods, and peeling or biting product off damages the nail plate. Tell us which system you currently wear when you book so the right method is prepared.

What is included

The acrylic menu

Acrylic extension

Additional length and structure, customisable in length and shape. Maintenance and safe removal are important — please do not peel extensions off at home.

Acrylic overlay

An acrylic coating over the natural nails without necessarily adding extension length — a structured surface while keeping the existing nail length.

When to book a refill

Book a refill before lifting starts, as the set grows out. Suitability depends on lifting, condition and the existing enhancement system — send a photo on WhatsApp if your set is already lifting.

Safe removal

Never bite, peel or forcibly remove extensions. Professional removal uses the technique suited to the extension material, so the natural nail plate stays protected.
