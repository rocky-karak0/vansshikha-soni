Nail Extensions

Nail Extensions

Length and structure — gel, acrylic, temporary, soft gel and gum gel extensions, each with the official rate shown.

All Services

Length, shape, structure and suitability are assessed by the nail technician first, so the result fits your natural nail condition as well as your style. Every set needs refills every 2–4 weeks plus professional removal at the end.

Temporary extensions suit occasions or clients who want a less permanent look, while gum gel is sculpted with a mouldable builder-style gel for a custom-shaped, structured finish. Exact materials and wear time are confirmed by the studio.

Bring a reference photo or describe the length and shape you have in mind. Final pricing is confirmed on WhatsApp, and nothing is booked until you receive a confirmation reply.

Extension types

Five ways to add length

Gel extension

M ₹900 / R ₹1,000 — added length with a shaped, finished look, built with an extension system and gel-based product.

Acrylic extension

M ₹1,100 / R ₹1,200 — additional length and structure with customisable length and shape. Maintenance and safe removal are important.

Temporary extension

M ₹700 / R ₹800 — a shorter-term nail-length option for occasions or clients seeking a less permanent look.

Soft gel extension

M ₹800 / R ₹900 — a smooth, shaped extension look using soft-gel tips or a related soft-gel system, in various lengths and styles.

Gum gel extension

M ₹1,500 / R ₹1,700 — sculpted with a mouldable builder-style gel for custom-shaped extensions with a structured finish.

Refills & removal

Extensions are usually refilled every 2–4 weeks. Professional removal uses the method suited to the material — never peel or bite product off at home.
